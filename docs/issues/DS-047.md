# DS-047 — Single-line editors sat high in their frame

Status: Resolved · 2026-11-07

## Problem

Reported by the user from the showcase: text inside `Input` and `TimePicker`
looked closer to the top of the frame than to the bottom. Measured at 2x on
the reference renderer with capital letters, which have no descender, a
medium input showed 27 pixels above the ink and 37 below; a large input 34
and 43; the time picker 27 and 37. The number input, which centers its editor
explicitly, measured 32 and 32.

The frame's vertical inner margin (`style::frame`) assumes a 1.5-em row, 24
points at the 16-point body size, while egui lays out a 16-point Inter row at
about 19.4 points and places a single-line editor's row at the top of its
content box by default. The unused 4.6 points therefore all fell below the
text.

## Remediation

Center the row in single-line editors with egui's own alignment instead of
changing the margin arithmetic, so the frame height, text size and padding
tokens stay as documented: `Input` aligns single-line rows to the vertical
center and keeps multi-line (`Textarea`) rows at the top; `TimePicker` centers
its row. `NumberInput` and `DatePicker` already centered theirs. Add a test
that compares the painted text center with the frame center at every control
size, and one that keeps textareas top-aligned.

## Resolution

`tests/editor_alignment.rs` failed before the change (text center 29.5 against
a frame center of 32 for the medium input, 22.5 against 24 for the small one)
and passes after it with the row within 0.75 points of the frame center at
Small, Medium and Large; the textarea stays at the top. Baselines that contain
single-line inputs were refreshed and inspected. Select triggers and buttons
were already centered and are unchanged.
