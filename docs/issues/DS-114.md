# DS-114 — InlineEdit input and StatusBar overflow

Status: Resolved in 1.4.0 · 2026-11-10

## Problem

A second opening click can activate a newly revealed rename action, and the
held Space guard can consume a fresh press after release or focus change.
StatusBar can return an out-of-bounds segment when its fixed parts exceed the
elision budget, and unconstrained status text can wrap at newlines.

## Remediation

Scope the opening gestures to the matching pointer sequence or owned keyboard
focus. Fit every part of a surviving status segment, report items that cannot
fit and normalize status text to one line. Add regressions for all four cases.

## Resolution

InlineEdit keeps the draft open and selected when an opening double-click
lands on Save or Cancel. A rapid click at a different position can still
save. The held Space guard stops on release or when focus leaves InlineEdit,
preserving a fresh press even if release and press arrive in the same frame.

StatusBar fits the whole segment within the bar and reports it as hidden
when even a compact text line cannot fit. Labels, values, and shortcuts
display line breaks as spaces. Elided segments retain their full wording in
the tooltip and keep their accessibility role and value.

Six new regression tests cover these cases and batched pointer motion. All
23 local checks passed, including 771 tests with all features and 518 without
default features. Rendering snapshots passed unchanged across themes and
display scales. In the macOS showcase, a native double-click left the name
selected; a separate Save click committed the replacement and returned focus
to the label.
