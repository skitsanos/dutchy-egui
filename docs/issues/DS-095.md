# DS-095 — Disabled and busy SplitButton shows a darker seam

Status: Resolved in 1.0.28 (found in its review) · Priority: P3 · 2026-11-09

## Problem

The arrow overlaps the primary by one border width so the borders join. In the
disabled and busy states both halves are faded, so the overlapping strip is
blended twice and reads as a darker full-height line at the arrow's leading
edge. The divider is painted outside the faded scope, so it stays at full
strength while the rest of the control fades. Flat fills are a design rule.

The new baselines show it in both themes. In the light 2x disabled row, the
fill is (250, 182, 133), the two-pixel seam is (250, 149, 77) and the divider
is the unfaded border token (231, 223, 218). The dark 1.5x baseline shows the
same seam as a bright orange line.

## Remediation

Paint one shared fill for the composite, or keep the overlap to borders only,
so no translucent fill is drawn twice. Fade the divider with the control's
disabled or busy state. Regenerate the three command-control baselines and
inspect light and dark at 1x, 1.5x and 2x, including hover and focus.

## Resolution

Only the primary paints and hit-tests the shared border strip, so faded fills
are blended once. The divider fades with disabled and busy states, and both
halves' focus rings are repainted over the joint. Focus inspection found a
second defect: a keyboard-focused Outline arrow drew its chevron in its own
fill colour. The chevron now follows the button's focused and pressed visuals.
Pixel regressions check one fill across the joint and a faded divider for
disabled and busy Primary and disabled Secondary, plus a visible focused Outline
chevron. The three command-control baselines were regenerated. Enabled, menu
and Todo baselines are unchanged. See the
[review record](../verification-review-1.0.28.md).
Included in the [1.0.28 release qualification](../verification-release-1.0.28.md).
