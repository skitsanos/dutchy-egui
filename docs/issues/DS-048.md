# DS-048 — The date picker's calendar opened under its button

Status: Resolved · 2026-11-07

## Problem

Reported by the user from the showcase: the calendar popup opened to the
right, aligned with the small calendar button at the end of the field rather
than with the field itself, and spilled over the neighboring column. The
popup was anchored to the button's response with egui's default start
alignment, so its left edge followed the button. In a narrow viewport egui
pushed the popup back inside the screen, which hid the defect from the
original 520-point test.

## Remediation

Anchor the popup to the whole field rectangle with bottom-start alignment and
keep the button registered as the popup's anchor widget so clicking it still
toggles rather than counting as a click outside. Widen the test viewport so
there is room to the right, then assert that the grid hangs below the field,
starts under the field rather than the button, and does not spill past the
field's right edge.

## Resolution

`tests/pickers.rs::calendar_opens_below_the_field_aligned_with_its_start`
failed on the anchored-to-button behavior (Monday column at x 460 under a
button at 442 to 486) and passes after the change. The grid now opens below
the field aligned with its start; egui's alternative alignments still apply
when the field is near the bottom or the right edge of the viewport. The
`primitives-calendar-1x` baseline was refreshed and inspected.
