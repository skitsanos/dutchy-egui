# DS-125 — Modal backdrop left the window edges undimmed and clickable

Status: Resolved (unreleased) · 2026-11-11

## Problem

A Modal dimmed the window except for a 16-point band along every edge, while
a Drawer dimmed all of it. The band showed in the Showcase Modal page and
over any host toolbar or sidebar that reaches the window edge.

`egui::Modal` paints its backdrop and places the backdrop's click target
inside its area, and an area clips both to its constraint rectangle. The
Modal constrained its area to the window shrunk by `VIEWPORT_GUTTER` to keep
the surface clear of the edges, so the backdrop was cut to the same
rectangle. The band was also not blocked: a click there missed the backdrop
and activated the host control underneath, with the dialog still open.

## Remediation

Reproduce the band in a rendering test before changing the library. Let the
area span the window so the backdrop covers and intercepts all of it. Keep
the surface clear of the edges through its width and height limits, which
already subtract the gutter on both sides of a centered surface. Check
CommandPalette and Lightbox for the same defect, and confirm that existing
Modal baselines change only inside the band.

## Resolution

The Modal no longer narrows its area; the surface limits alone keep the
16-point clearance. Focus confinement, dismissal reasons and the dialog's
accessible name and role are unchanged.

`tests/modal_backdrop.rs` samples the corners and edge midpoints of the band
in light 1× and dark 2×. Before the change a corner read the plain
background `[250, 248, 245]` beside a dimmed `[134, 132, 128]`, the backdrop
target measured 768 × 568 in an 800 × 600 window, and an edge click reached
the host. After it every sample matches the rest of the backdrop, an edge
click is blocked when backdrop dismissal is off and reports
`OverlayDismissal::Backdrop` when it is on, and the surface stays inside the
gutter at 800 × 600 and 280 × 360 with its height limit intact.

CommandPalette and Lightbox build their own areas over the full window and
pass the same checks; they needed no change. The four Modal baselines were
refreshed after a pixel comparison found every changed pixel inside the
band at 1×, 1.5× and 2×.

The change was verified headlessly. It was not checked in a native window.
