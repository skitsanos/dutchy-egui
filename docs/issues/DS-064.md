# DS-064 — Hovered unpressed Outline toggles look pressed

Status: Resolved in 1.0.24 · 2026-11-08

## Problem

`ToggleButton` defaults to the Outline variant and reuses `ButtonVariant::visuals`.
The Outline hover treatment inverts the surface (foreground fill, background
ink), which is also the resting surface of a pressed toggle (Secondary). Hovering
an unpressed toggle therefore paints exactly the pressed state, and a pressed
toggle switches to the accent (Primary) on hover. A pointer user cannot tell
"off, hovered" from "on, resting", and the accent hover reads as a primary
action. Toolbar items using the Outline variant behave the same; Ghost items,
the toolbar default, are unaffected because their hover is the muted fill.

Measured on 2026-11-08 with a temporary egui_kittest harness (Orange/light):
pressed rest fill `#120F0CFF`, unpressed hover fill `#120F0CFF`, pressed hover
fill `#E06813E6`.

## Remediation

Keep hover feedback inside each state. Unpressed Outline and Ghost toggles hover
with the muted fill and foreground stroke; pressed toggles keep the inverse
surface and tint it (the 0.9 gamma already used by Secondary hover, or
`tokens::actions::PRESSED_TINT`) instead of switching to the accent. Record the
state table in `docs/actions-toolbars.md`, add a rendering regression asserting
that the unpressed hover fill differs from the pressed rest fill and that the
pressed hover fill is not the accent, and refresh the `actions-*` baselines,
which hover the Italic toolbar item.

## Resolution

`actions::core::surface` now selects the hover surface: an unpressed Outline
toggle hovers with Ghost's muted fill plus a foreground stroke, and a pressed
toggle always uses Secondary (foreground fill, tinted by the existing 0.9 gamma
while hovered) instead of switching to Primary. Plain IconButtons keep the
ordinary button hover. The regression
`toggle_hover_keeps_pressed_and_unpressed_surfaces_distinct`
(`tests/actions_rendering.rs`) asserts that the hovered off fill is the muted
fill with a foreground stroke and differs from the pressed rest fill, and that
the pressed hover fill is the tinted foreground rather than the accent; it
failed before the change. The `actions-*` baselines hover a Ghost toolbar item
and are unchanged. The actions guide records the state table. Evidence
for the original defect: `src/widgets/button.rs`
(`Self::Outline if hovered => (p.foreground, p.background, p.foreground)`), the
former pressed-and-hovered `Primary` selection in `src/widgets/actions/core.rs`,
and the fills measured on 2026-11-08 (pressed rest `#120F0CFF`, unpressed hover
`#120F0CFF`, pressed hover `#E06813E6`).
